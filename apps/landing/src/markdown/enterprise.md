# Lunaris for enterprise

Lunaris Enterprise brings offline-first writing and collaboration to organizations that need centralized access controls.

## Security and compliance

Lunaris encrypts synced workspace data in transit and at rest. The [Lunaris Trust Center](https://trust.lunaris.app) lists [security documents](https://trust.lunaris.app/documents) and [subprocessors](https://trust.lunaris.app/subprocessors). You can request access to restricted documents there.

## Organization access

- **Single sign-on (SSO).** Employees sign in through your identity provider.
- **SCIM.** Automate account provisioning from your identity provider.
- **Custom roles.** Define the permissions each group needs.

## Data residency and extensions

Eligible Enterprise organizations can choose where their Lunaris Cloud data is hosted.

Use the [Lunaris Extensions SDK](https://docs.lunaris.app/developers/extensions/quickstart) to build private tools for your organization, such as admin panels and project trackers.

[Compare plans and limits](https://lunaris.app/pricing.md)

## Rollout questions

- **Can we host Lunaris ourselves?** Run [Community Edition](https://lunaris.app/self-hosted.md) on your infrastructure with Docker Compose.
- **Does Lunaris work offline?** You can keep writing offline. Synced work catches up when you reconnect.
- **Does Lunaris train AI models on our writing?** Lunaris does not use your writing to train AI models.
