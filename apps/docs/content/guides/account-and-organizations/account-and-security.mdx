# Account and Security

Open your user menu and choose **Settings** to manage your profile and sign-in security.

## Account

The **Account** tab lets you change your display name and profile image, review your email address, and see the data region used by your account.

If you started as a guest, claim the account with email or Google before relying on cloud sync or moving between browsers. Existing Local workspaces stay on this device after sign-up. Export important local work before clearing data.

The danger zone lets you request account deletion. Read the confirmation carefully: deleting an account is different from deleting one workspace or leaving an organization.

## Security

The **Security** tab can include:

- setting or changing a password;
- connecting or disconnecting Google as a sign-in method;
- adding passkeys when supported;
- enabling two-factor authentication with an authenticator app;
- regenerating two-factor backup codes;
- reviewing active sessions and revoking other devices.

Keep more than one safe way to sign in before disconnecting a provider. When you enable two-factor authentication, save the backup codes somewhere separate from the device running your authenticator.

If you see a session you do not recognize, revoke it and change your password. Security details require a connection to load and update.
