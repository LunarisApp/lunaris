# Work Offline

Lunaris saves work on your device so you can keep editing when your connection drops.

## Prepare before going offline

Open the cloud workspace—and any uploaded files you will need—while connected. Wait for its initial download and confirm the sync indicator is healthy. Content that has never reached this browser cannot be fetched without a connection.

You can also install the web app from a supported browser. See [Web, Desktop, and Mobile](/getting-started/web-desktop-and-mobile).

## While offline

Continue opening downloaded content and making edits. Lunaris saves changes locally and shows **You Are Offline**. Features that need a server, such as invitations, billing, organization activity, backups, and the assistant, remain unavailable until you reconnect.

## When you reconnect

Keep Lunaris open while it uploads local changes and downloads newer cloud changes. Wait until the status returns to online and synced before clearing browser data or signing out on a shared device.

Local workspaces never sync. They remain in the browser or app where they were created, whether you are online or offline.
