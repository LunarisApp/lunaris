# Preferences and Accessibility

Lunaris can adapt its appearance and controls without changing the content of your workspaces.

## Change appearance and language

Open your user menu, select **Settings**, then **Preferences**. You can choose:

- the Lunaris interface language;
- **System**, **Light**, or **Dark** theme;
- compact, large, or extra-large interface text;
- default, serif, or dyslexia-friendly type.

Changes apply immediately in the current browser. **System** follows the appearance setting of your device.

## Use the keyboard

Open the **Keybindings** tab in Settings to review shortcuts available in your current app and platform. Frequently used shortcuts include:

- <kbd>⌘</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> for search;
- <kbd>⌘</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>,</kbd> for Settings;
- <kbd>F2</kbd> to rename a selected workspace resource;
- <kbd>⌘</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>F</kbd> for Focus Mode.

Lunaris supports keyboard navigation for menus, dialogs, the workspace tree, and drag-and-drop organization. Browser zoom can provide additional scaling beyond the built-in text sizes.

The **Debug logging** option is intended for troubleshooting. Leave it off unless you are diagnosing a problem or support asks you to enable it.
