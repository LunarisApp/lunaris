# Invite People and Share Workspaces

Collaboration starts at the organization level. Invite someone to the organization, then use each workspace's visibility to decide what they can access.

## Invite a teammate

Organization owners can:

1. Open **Organization Settings** from the organization selector.
2. Go to **Members**.
3. Enter the person's email address and choose **Editor** or **Viewer**.
4. Select **Send invite**.

Pending invitations appear on the same page. An owner can resend or cancel one before it is accepted or expires. Personal organizations do not support additional members; create a shared organization first.

## Share a workspace

Open **Workspace Settings** and choose its visibility:

- **Shared** makes the cloud workspace available to every member of the organization, according to their organization role.
- **Private** limits the workspace to its owner and organization members who receive workspace access.
- **Local** stays in the current browser and cannot be shared or synced.

For a private workspace, its owner can add eligible organization members as editors or viewers from the same settings page. The person must already belong to the organization.

See [Roles and Permissions](/guides/collaboration/roles-and-permissions) before assigning access.
