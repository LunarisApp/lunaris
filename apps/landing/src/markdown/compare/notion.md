# Lunaris vs Notion

Lunaris supports offline editing across workspaces and can keep a workspace local to one device. Extensions add tools per workspace, and authorized MCP clients can connect to Lunaris.

Notion organizes work around pages and databases. Its apps support offline use for downloaded pages.

See [Lunaris workspace visibility](https://docs.lunaris.app/reference/workspace-visibility) for how local storage, sync, and access differ by workspace.
