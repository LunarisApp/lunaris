# Manage an organization

Create an organization for people who should share membership, billing, and administrative controls. Within it, use Private workspaces to restrict a project to selected members.

## Create or switch organizations

1. Open the organization selector above **Recent** and choose **Create new**.
2. Enter a name and a unique slug. The slug becomes part of organization links. Changing it later can make bookmarks outdated.
3. Select the new organization, then open **Organization Settings**. Use **Members** to [invite your team](/teams/share-a-workspace).

A Personal organization is for one person and does not have a Members tab. Switch organizations before managing settings so you know which one you are changing.

## Review billing and storage

As an owner, open **Organization Settings → Billing** to see the live plan, limits, member count, and subscription details. Use **Manage billing** to open the billing portal when available.

Cloud workspaces, including Private ones, share the organization's storage allowance. Uploaded files in Trash continue to use cloud storage until permanently deleted. Local files do not count against the cloud allowance. If uploads stop because storage is full, review the live limits before freeing space or changing plans.

Before deleting an organization, [download a backup](/administration/audit-and-backup) and separately export important Local work. Resource Trash cannot restore a deleted organization.
