# Manage Organizations

An organization groups workspaces and people. Create separate organizations for different teams or clients.

## Switch or create an organization

Use the organization selector above **Recent Workspaces** to switch between organizations. The workspace list changes to the selected organization.

To create another one:

1. Open the organization selector.
2. Choose **Create new**.
3. Enter a name and a unique organization slug.

The slug becomes part of organization links. Choose a stable, recognizable value; changing it can make previously copied links outdated.

## Change organization settings

Select the settings button beside the organization selector. The available tabs depend on your role and plan:

- **General** for the organization name and slug;
- **Members** for invitations and roles;
- **Billing**, **Backups**, and **Audit log** for owner controls.

Personal organizations are intended for one person and do not have a Members tab. Create a regular organization when you want to collaborate.

Only an owner can make administrative changes or delete an organization. Organization deletion affects every workspace it owns, so download a [backup](/guides/offline-and-sync/back-up-an-organization) first.
