# Plans and Billing

Plans apply to the active organization. Open **Organization Settings → Billing** to see its current plan, subscription status, renewal or end date, and seat information.

## Choose a plan

Organization owners can compare the plans currently available to that organization. Switch between monthly and annual billing to compare the effective per-member price, then choose a plan to continue to secure checkout.

Prices, features, and limits can change, so use the values shown on the Billing page as the current source of truth. A feature may also show an upgrade prompt when the organization reaches its plan limit.

## Organization storage

Each organization shares one storage allowance across all its workspaces, including private workspaces: Free 250 MB, Plus 10 GB, Business 100 GB, and Enterprise 1 TB unless its contract specifies otherwise. Adding or removing members does not change this allowance. Private workspaces remain organization-owned.

Files in Trash still count toward storage until permanently deleted. Local files do not use cloud storage.

When storage is full, free space or upgrade to upload more files. Existing files remain available to read, download, and delete. Text changes continue to sync. Individual files also have a size limit; check your plan before uploading a large file.

## Manage an existing subscription

Select **Manage billing** to open the billing portal. Depending on the subscription, you can update payment details, review invoices, change the plan, or cancel renewal there.

Only organization owners can update billing. Editors and viewers do not have access to these controls.

Billing needs an internet connection. If Lunaris is offline, it may show the last verified plan information, but purchases and subscription changes stay disabled until live verification returns.
