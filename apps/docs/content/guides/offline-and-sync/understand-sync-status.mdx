# Understand Sync Status

The sync indicator shows the cloud connection and any transfers in progress. Select it for details such as the last successful sync.

## Common states

- **Cloud sync connected** — Lunaris is connected. Check pending uploads before closing the app.
- **Uploading** or **Downloading** — changes or files are moving; keep Lunaris open.
- **You Are Offline** — cloud-workspace edits stay on this device until you reconnect.
- **Cloud Sync Is Off** — you are using a guest account. Local workspaces also stay on this device, regardless of the connection status.
- **Sync Interrupted** — a temporary failure occurred; Lunaris retries, or you can choose **Retry Sync**.
- **Sign In to Resume Sync** — the session expired. Sign in again, then retry.
- **Sync Needs Access** — the current account cannot upload the queued change. Review access or billing.
- **Sync Paused** — repeated uploads were rejected and automatic attempts stopped.

For a paused state, open **Settings → Local Data** and choose **Retry Sync** after correcting the cause. **Discard Pending Uploads** is a last resort: it permanently discards pending uploads and can lose unsynced work.

An online network icon alone does not guarantee every change has synced. Before closing the app after important work, wait for transfers to finish and check the last synced time.
