# Navigate Lunaris

## Find a workspace

On **Recent Workspaces**, choose an organization, then select a workspace to open
it. Search by name, sort by activity or name, and switch between grid and list views.
Use **Create** for a new workspace or an Obsidian import.

The account menu opens app settings and lets you switch accounts or sign out.
**Agent Work** opens a conversation sidebar and chat in the desktop app. Connect your ChatGPT account to ask about workspace resources, draft content, and review proposed changes before approving them. Conversations are saved on your device. An eligible ChatGPT subscription is required.

**Chats** is marked **Coming soon**.

## Inside a workspace

- **Top bar:** switch workspaces, open workspace settings, search, or manage extensions.
- **Workspace sidebar:** find folders and resources. Use **Add file**, **Add Folder**,
  or the search field. Right-click a resource for more actions.
- **Trash:** recover resources deleted from the sidebar within 30 days.
- **Tabs and panels:** switch between open resources or arrange them side by side.
- **Sync indicator:** check the cloud connection and pending transfers.

See [Tabs, Panels, and Windows](/guides/organizing-workspaces/tabs-panels-and-windows)
for layout controls.

## Search and shortcuts

Press `⌘ K` on macOS or `Ctrl K` on Windows and Linux to find workspaces,
resources, document text, and commands. Use the arrow keys to select a result,
`Enter` to open it, and `Esc` to close search.

Other useful shortcuts:

- `⌘ N` / `Ctrl N`: open a new tab.
- `⌘ ,` / `Ctrl ,`: open app settings.
- `⌘ Shift F` / `Ctrl Shift F`: toggle Focus Mode.
- `F2`: rename the selected resource.

View or change shortcuts in **Settings → Keybindings**.

## On a phone or tablet

The web app shows one view at a time. Select a resource to open it, then use
Back to return to the workspace.
