# Sync Problems

Lunaris saves edits locally first, then syncs cloud workspaces when a connection
is available. A pending upload does not mean the edit is lost, but it has not
yet reached Lunaris Cloud or your other devices.

## Changes are not appearing on another device

On the device where you made the change:

1. Confirm the workspace is **Private** or **Shared**. Local workspaces never sync.
2. Open Lunaris and reconnect to the internet.
3. Select the sync indicator and wait for **Uploading** to finish.
4. Open **Settings → Local Data** and confirm the **Upload Queue** returns to no
   pending uploads.

On the other device, confirm you selected the same account, data region,
organization, and workspace. Keep the workspace open while **Downloading** is shown.

## Sync Interrupted

**Sync Interrupted** means a transfer failed temporarily and Lunaris is retrying.
Keep the app open and check that your connection is stable. Networks with a
sign-in page, such as hotel or public Wi-Fi, may appear online before internet
access is complete.

If the error remains after reconnecting, open **Settings → Local Data** and
review the shown reason and upload queue. Reload Lunaris once, then allow it to
retry.

## Sign In to Resume Sync

Your session expired and cloud uploads were rejected.

1. Choose **Sign In** from the sync message.
2. Sign in to the same account and data region.
3. Return to **Settings → Local Data**.
4. Choose **Retry Sync**, then wait for the upload queue to clear.

Do not continue as a new guest account: it will not restore access to the
original account's cloud workspaces.

## Sync Needs Access

The server rejected a queued change under the current plan or permissions.
Check whether:

- your organization or private-workspace role changed;
- you now have Viewer access instead of Editor access;
- the workspace or organization was removed; or
- the organization plan needs attention.

Ask the organization or workspace owner to restore the needed access. Organization
owners can review billing from **Organization Settings → Billing**. After the cause
is fixed, open **Settings → Local Data** and choose **Retry Sync**.

## Sync Paused

Lunaris pauses automatic attempts after repeated upload rejections. The pending
changes stay in this browser unless you clear them.

1. Correct the sign-in, permission, plan, or connection problem shown.
2. Open **Settings → Local Data**.
3. Choose **Retry Sync**.
4. Keep Lunaris open until no pending uploads remain.

## Last Synced does not change

**Last Synced** records the last completed cloud sync. It will not advance for
a Local workspace, while offline, or while transfers are still pending. Check the
upload queue and wait for both **Uploading** and **Downloading** to finish.

## Last-resort local controls

Use these only after normal retries fail:

- **Discard Pending Uploads** permanently discards queued uploads and their unsynced
  data. It does not upload or repair them.
- **Purge All Local Data** deletes Lunaris databases and downloaded files from
  this browser, then reloads the app. Synced cloud data can download again;
  Local workspaces and unsynced changes cannot be recovered from the cloud.

See [Manage Local Data](/guides/offline-and-sync/manage-local-data) before using
either action.
