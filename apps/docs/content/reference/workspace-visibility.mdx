# Workspace visibility

Choose visibility when creating a workspace. It determines storage, sync, and access.

| Visibility | Where it lives | Who can open it | Sync and backups |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Local** | The browser profile or desktop app where you created it | People with access to that app or profile on the device | No sync, no cloud backup |
| **Private** | Local data on each device and synced cloud storage | The workspace owner and organization members explicitly granted access | Syncs across devices. Synced content can be backed up. |
| **Shared** | Local data on each device and synced cloud storage | Every member of the organization, subject to their role | Syncs across devices. Synced content can be backed up. |

## Local

Guests use Local. Signed-in users can also choose Local. Signing in on another device or moving from the browser to the desktop app does not bring the workspace with you. A Local workspace cannot be switched to Private or Shared in Workspace Settings. [Export important work](/personal/export-your-work) before clearing browser data or changing devices.

## Private

Private keeps a synced workspace restricted. Its owner can grant an individual organization member **Editor** or **Viewer** access. Private workspaces still belong to the organization and count toward its cloud storage.

## Shared

Shared makes the workspace available to the whole organization. Each person's organization role determines what they can do. See the [roles and permissions matrix](/reference/roles-and-permissions).

## Changing Private and Shared

With the required permission, use **Workspace Settings → Visibility** to switch between Private and Shared. Switching to Private removes organization-wide access. [Grant access](/teams/share-a-workspace) to the members who should still be able to open it.
