# Lunaris Docs

Lunaris brings your writing, notes, files, and plans into one place. It saves
changes as you work and lets you keep writing when your connection drops.

New here? [Create your first workspace](/getting-started).

## Find what you need

- **Write:** [Create and format a document](/guides/writing-and-editing/create-and-format-documents), then [export or copy it](/guides/writing-and-editing/export-and-copy-documents).
- **Organize:** [Use folders](/guides/organizing-workspaces/folders-and-workspace-tree) or [search your work](/guides/organizing-workspaces/search).
- **Share:** [Invite people and choose their access](/guides/collaboration/invite-and-share).
- **Work offline:** [Prepare your workspace](/guides/offline-and-sync/work-offline) and [check sync status](/guides/offline-and-sync/understand-sync-status).
- **Add tools:** [Manage extensions](/guides/tools-and-extensions/manage-extensions) for boards, drawings, files, and the assistant.
- **Get help:** [Fix common problems](/troubleshooting).

## Three terms to know

An **organization** groups your workspaces and the people you work with.
A **workspace** holds one project, such as a book, course, or client job.
A **resource** is content inside a workspace: a folder, document, board, task,
drawing, or uploaded file.

Choose [Local, Private, or Shared](/getting-started/local-private-and-shared) for
each workspace to decide where it is saved and who can open it.

For custom tools and connections, visit [Developers](/developers).
