# Workspace Visibility

Every workspace has one visibility setting. It controls where the workspace is
stored and who can open it.

| Visibility | Cloud sync | Access | Can change visibility? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Local** | No | Anyone with access to this browser or desktop app on this device | No |
| **Private** | Yes | Workspace owner and organization members granted access | Yes, to Shared |
| **Shared** | Yes | Every member of the organization | Yes, to Private |

## Local

Local workspaces keep their content and uploaded files in the browser or desktop app where you created them. They do not appear on another device after sign-in, cannot be shared,
and are not included in cloud organization backups.

Clearing site data can remove a Local workspace. Export your writing and download important files before clearing
local data or replacing your device. A Local
workspace cannot later be converted to Private or Shared.

## Private

Private workspaces sync through Lunaris Cloud but remain restricted. The workspace
owner can grant individual organization members **Editor** or **Viewer** access.
Someone must join the organization before they can receive private-workspace access.

Use Private for personal drafts or work meant for selected people.

## Shared

Shared workspaces sync through Lunaris Cloud and are available to every member of
the organization. Each member's organization role controls whether they can change
the workspace or only view it.

Use Shared for team knowledge and work everyone in the organization should find.

## Changing Private and Shared

A cloud workspace's visibility can change between Private and Shared from
**Workspace Settings → Visibility**. Changing a Shared workspace to Private removes
organization-wide access; grant selected members access afterward if they should
keep using it.
