# Use AI with your work

Choose the smallest access that fits the task. To summarize one draft, send that document. To ask questions across a workspace over time, use Luna or approve a connection to your own AI tool.

## Send one document

Open a Rich Text document and choose **More → Open in → ChatGPT** or **Claude**. With a longer document, Lunaris may copy the text to your clipboard for you to paste. The service receives the document text you send. Workspace access requires a separate connection.

## Ask Luna about selected resources

Enable the **Assistant** extension and open **Assistant** from the workspace tools. Type `@` in your message and select the workspace, folder, or resource you want Luna to use. Review any proposed change before approving it in the chat. The assistant needs an internet connection and its availability may depend on your organization plan.

## Connect an external AI app

Use MCP to let your AI app search or summarize across a workspace. Start with
read-only access. Follow the setup for Lunaris Cloud or the desktop app in
[Connect an AI app with MCP](/customize/mcp).
