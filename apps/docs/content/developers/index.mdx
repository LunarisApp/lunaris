# Developers

## Connect an AI tool

A connection lets an approved AI app read or work with your Lunaris content.
You choose its access, and your workspace permissions still apply.

For a walkthrough, see [Connect ChatGPT](/guides/tools-and-extensions/connect-chatgpt).
For other tools, see [Connect through MCP](/developers/mcp).

## Build an extension

Build a workspace tool or a new kind of content with TypeScript, React, and the
public SDK. [Build Extensions](/developers/extensions) covers the full workflow:

- [Create your first extension](/developers/extensions/quickstart).
- [Build a persistent resource and its editor](/developers/extensions/resources-and-views).
- [Store and migrate data](/developers/extensions/storage-and-migrations).
- [Test](/developers/extensions/testing) and [publish a release](/developers/extensions/publishing).

To use an existing extension, follow [Manage Extensions](/guides/tools-and-extensions/manage-extensions).
