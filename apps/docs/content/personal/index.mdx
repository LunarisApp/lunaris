# Personal work

Use a **Local** workspace for work you want to keep in one browser or desktop app. Use **Private** when you want your own work to sync across devices. Check [Workspace visibility](/reference/workspace-visibility) before creating a workspace.

- [Write and organize](/personal/write-and-organize) a project with folders, search, and side-by-side views.
- [Work offline](/personal/work-offline) and prepare a synced workspace before losing your connection.
- [Import from Obsidian](/personal/import-from-obsidian) and check which links, files, and properties carry over.
- [Export your work](/personal/export-your-work) to keep a portable copy or assemble a PDF.
