# Share a workspace

Sharing takes two decisions. The organization controls who belongs to the team. The workspace controls which of those people can open the project.

## Invite someone to the organization

As an owner of a team organization:

1. Open **Organization Settings → Members**.
2. Enter the person's email and choose **Editor** or **Viewer**. Choose Viewer if they only need to read Shared work.
3. Select **Send invite**. Check the same page for pending invitations and resend or cancel an invitation if needed.

If you're in a Personal organization, [create a team organization](/administration/manage-organization) first. If the invitation fails, check [Account and access problems](/reference/account-and-access#an-invitation-cannot-be-accepted).

## Give them workspace access

- For a **Shared** workspace, the accepted organization invitation already grants access. The member's organization role controls what they can do.
- For a **Private** workspace, wait for the person to join the organization. Then, as the workspace owner, open **Workspace Settings** and grant that member **Editor** or **Viewer** access. This role applies to that workspace.

Send the workspace link after granting access. The link itself does not grant permission. Work created in a Local workspace must be taken out of it, for example by [exporting a document](/personal/export-your-work), before you can share a copy.

If you switch a Shared workspace to Private, review who still needs access and grant it explicitly. See [Roles and permissions](/reference/roles-and-permissions) for the access matrix.
