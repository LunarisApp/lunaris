# Account and access problems

Check the account and data region first. Once signed in, access depends on organization membership and workspace visibility.

## You cannot sign in

Use the original data region and the sign-in method shown for the account:

- For email and password, choose **Forgot password?** and open the newest reset email. If verification is required, check spam or resend the verification email after the cooldown.
- For a Google account without a Lunaris password, use **Continue with Google**.
- Use a device or password manager that has the passkey. For two-factor authentication, use your authenticator or **Use a backup code**.

If **Sign In to Resume Sync** appears, sign in to the same account and then [retry sync](/reference/recover-local-work#sync-is-paused-or-rejects-changes). A new guest session cannot restore access to the account's cloud workspaces.

## An invitation cannot be accepted

Use the exact address that received the invitation. Verify the address if Lunaris asks, then reopen the invitation. If the link was used, canceled, or expired, ask the organization owner to resend it and confirm there is room to add a member.

## A workspace is missing or read-only

Open the current organization from the organization selector and find the workspace from its workspace list. A bookmark can become outdated after a rename, or the workspace may have changed from Shared to Private.

- For **Shared** work, ask the organization owner to check your membership and organization role.
- For **Private** work, ask the workspace owner to confirm your workspace access after you join the organization.
- For **Local** work, return to the original browser profile or desktop app.

If you can open a workspace but cannot edit, compare the [organization and workspace roles](/reference/roles-and-permissions).

## Review security settings

In **Settings → Security**, review available sign-in methods, passkeys, two-factor authentication, and active sessions. Save backup codes separately from your authenticator. If you do not recognize a session, revoke it and review your sign-in methods.
